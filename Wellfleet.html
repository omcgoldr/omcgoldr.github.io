Wellfleet, Massachusetts

Where is Wellfleet?

Wellfleet is located on the outer Cape, just two towns away from the tip of the Cape Cod arm. Much of the town is protected by the National Seashore, meaning that undeveloped land is preserved by law.

What's the weather like in Wellfleet?

Wellfleet's temperature varies significantly throughout the year: the winters are very cold and the summers are generally hot and humid. Julys are particularly warm but the temperature starts to cool by late August.

See today's weather forecasthere.

What's Wellfleet's population?

Wellfleet's population fluctuates heavily between the on- and off-season. During the winters, the population is only about 4,000 or so. In the summers, the population is almost 6 times as much.
